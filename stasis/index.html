STASIS

A tape deck for slowing music down. Throw the lever to any speed, from a crawl to a stop to reverse, and the pitch stays where you left it. Or flip to Classic and let it fall like real tape.

macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon. One serial for your own Macs.

Secure checkout by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. Your serial arrives on this page and by email.

Thank you!

Making your serial...

Your serial:

Download Stasis, open it, paste the serial into the box and press Activate. A copy is on its way to your email.

Any speed, same pitch

Slow a passage to a tenth, hold it, rock it back and forth by the reels. The music keeps its key.

Or real tape

Classic mode lets the pitch fall with the speed, all the way down to a stop and into reverse.

Hands on

A speed lever, a bend wheel, reels you can grab, tape you can drag, a head gap and tape high end to shade the sound.

Bring your own audio

Drop in a WAV. A demo tape ships in the box so there is something to play right away.
