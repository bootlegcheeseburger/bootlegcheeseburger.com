Privacy

Stasis by Bootleg Cheeseburger. Last updated [DATE].

The short version

Stasis has no analytics, no tracking and no ads. We hold only what it takes to sell you a serial and keep it working.

When you buy

Paddle, our reseller, handles the checkout and payment and processes your name, email, address and payment details under its own privacy policy. Paddle passes us your email and the order's id.

What we keep

  • Your email and order id, attached to your serial in our licensing service (Keygen), to deliver the serial and answer support requests.
  • For each Mac you activate: a one-way hash of the Mac's hardware id, the Mac's name and its platform, so a serial can count its seats.

We send your serial by email through [Resend]. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

What Stasis sends

When you activate, and occasionally afterwards, Stasis sends your serial and the hashed hardware id to Keygen to check the licence. Your audio never leaves your Mac.

Your choices

Ask us at dan@dantimmons.net to see, correct or delete what we hold. Deleting it ends the serial.
