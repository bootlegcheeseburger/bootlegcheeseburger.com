MoAir

AirPods Max, but pro.

A menu-bar utility that turns AirPods Max into a head-tracking, dB-calibrated, professional-quality audio interface. Instant OSC connections to every popular head-tracking app, plus fine-tuning - one click and you're patched in. Also works with AirPods (buds) without the calibration.

FloCha

See your session, finally.

A signal-flow visualizer for Pro Tools. Tracks, buses, sends, VCAs, group memberships - rendered as a flowchart so you can spot dead ends and mis-mappings before the mixer does.

StreamPEC

PEC-Direct for the rest of us.

Instantly turns your Streamdeck XL into PEC paddles. Talks to the Streamdeck and Pro Tools directly with no extra bloat or messy config. Just run it and put your Pro Tools record tracks in a folder called "print" (or configure your own template). Boom. Punched.
