NoteTaken

Timecoded notes for film post.

ADR, Foley, music, background, custom cues - all anchored to timecode, all in one .notek bundle. Pro Tools PTSL integration. Local-first. Built for editors and supervisors who keep losing their notes in a thousand text files.

MoAir

AirPods Max, but pro.

A menu-bar utility that turns AirPods Max into a head-tracking, dB-calibrated, codec-readout pro-audio rig. Live OSC out, USB-C wired tracking, and a connection panel that finally tells you what you're actually listening to.

macOS · Open beta

FloCha

See your session, finally.

A signal-flow visualizer for Pro Tools. Tracks, buses, sends, VCAs, group memberships - rendered as a flowchart so you can spot dead ends and mis-mappings before the mix engineer does.

plupdate

Plugin updates, finally sane.

Scans your installed audio plugins, cross-checks a crowdsourced database, and tells you what's actually out of date. No trawling twenty vendor sites. No "is iLok still online?" Just a list.
