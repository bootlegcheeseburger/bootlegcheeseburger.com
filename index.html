Stasis

Time flies.

Meet the world's first AAW /aw/ noun Analog audio workstation. Like a DAW, minus the D. as in: “aww, it sounds like real tape.” . A real virtual tape deck, powered by a completely unique audio algorithm that I dreamed up back in music school. Our proprietary Stasis Engine™ allows authentic analog playback in the digital domain, for infinite realtime varispeed with zero pitch change. Oh, and it sounds incredible.
Plus, you can play with the tape.

MoAir

AirPods Max, but pro.

StreamPEC

PEC-Direct for the rest of us.

Instantly turns your Streamdeck XL into PEC paddles. Talks to the Streamdeck and Pro Tools directly with no extra bloat or messy config. Just run it and put your Pro Tools record tracks in a folder called "print" (or configure your own template). Boom. Punched.
